About

Honjobs brings together the many sides of Sihyun Kyung: mathematics instructor, kendo practitioner, licensed tourist interpreter guide, mission partner, and creator of AI-powered tools.

Since 2008, I have taught calculus, statistics, linear algebra, and data analysis at around 20 universities in Seoul and the surrounding area. My studies in mathematics at Myongji University, a mathematics master’s degree at the University of Houston, and a master’s in applied data science at the University of Michigan bring mathematics and data together in my work.

Outside the classroom, I have practiced kendo since 1992 and attained 5th dan, guided travelers as a licensed tourist interpreter guide, and served in mission fields in Chad, Thailand, and Sri Lanka. These days, I build apps and AI tools for churches and classrooms.
